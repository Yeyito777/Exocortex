chrono is this conversation's scheduler. Use it instead of Bash sleep, cron, or polling loops to wait, sleep, or schedule future work. A wake with a message starts a new turn in this conversation at the scheduled time, after this turn has ended and across restarts. Sleeps and waits of any practical length run inside the call and end early when the user sends a message or one of your background tasks finishes. Prefer wait on the exact task, unit:NAME (systemd user unit) or subagent over a fixed sleep; when sleeping on running work, sleep no longer than it likely needs, at most ~5–10 min per check.
