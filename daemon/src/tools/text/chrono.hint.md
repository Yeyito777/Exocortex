chrono is this conversation's scheduler: use it instead of shell sleep, cron, or polling loops to wait, sleep, or schedule future work. Use native session polling for shell tools that provide it. `wait` needs a `max_wait` safety limit and returns as soon as the task finishes: prefer it on the exact task, unit:NAME (systemd user unit) or subagent over a fixed sleep. A sleep or wait ends early when the user sends a message, and a sleep when one of your background tasks finishes. When sleeping on running work, sleep no longer than it likely needs, at most ~5–10 min per check. A wake persists across daemon restarts; one with a message starts a new turn in this conversation at the scheduled time, after this turn has ended.
